# The Creative Workstation is a separate system from the Video Orchestrator

The Video Orchestrator is a single-user, local AI production planner; the workstation is a multi-user workflow for the Marketing and Creative Teams. Although both have briefs, versions of a video, approval and plans for Meta data, we keep them as separate systems with separate models: the workstation does not depend on the orchestrator, and Meta performance data has one home, the workstation. If the two are connected later, the link is the Creative Code, with the orchestrator acting only as a production tool that delivers files into a Variant.

The workstation lives in its own repository, so the two similar-but-different vocabularies (Brief vs Content Brief, Variant vs Deliverable Variant) never share one codebase.
