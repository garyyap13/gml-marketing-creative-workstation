# Creative Workstation

The shared workspace where the Marketing Team and the Creative Team take a creative from brief to a live post or ad, review how it performs on Meta, and plan what to make next.

## Language

### People

**Marketing Team**:
The people who decide who a creative is for and what it says, approve it, and decide where it is posted and advertised.
_Avoid_: Marketing (as a person), client

**Creative Team**:
The people who decide how a creative is told and cut, and who make every revision.
_Avoid_: Editors, production, designers

**Approver**:
The named Marketing Team member whose decision approves a Version.
_Avoid_: Reviewer (anyone can review; only the Approver approves)

**Marketing Owner**:
The Marketing Team member responsible for a Brief.

**Creative Owner**:
The Creative Team member responsible for a Creative and its due date.
_Avoid_: Assignee, editor

### Making a creative

**Brief**:
The Marketing Team's starting point for one or more Creatives: its Product, Avatar, Angle, Funnel Stage, optional Initiative, key message and CTA, an optional hypothesis of what the team wants to learn, and optional attachments such as a script, references or a rough cut.
_Avoid_: 初稿 (the rough material attached to a Brief), request, ticket

**Rebrief**:
A new version of a Brief made after it was handed to the Creative Team; each Creative keeps the Brief version it followed.

**Creative**:
One distinct execution of a Brief, with its own Format and Narrative Style, together with every Variant made from it.
_Avoid_: Content, asset, deliverable, ad, concept

**Creative Code**:
The permanent, never-reused identifier of a Creative, issued when the Creative is created: the brand prefix plus a sequence number, e.g. `GML-0007`.
_Avoid_: Content code

**Legacy Code**:
A creative code in the older brand-type-number form, e.g. `GML-TESTI-0007`, still accepted so past ads keep matching.

**Legacy Creative**:
A Creative created from a past ad's Legacy Code, with no Brief and with labels filled in later.

**Variant**:
One specific cut of a Creative, such as a given length, aspect ratio or Hook, identified by its Creative Code plus a letter, e.g. `GML-0007-B`.
_Avoid_: Version, cut, edit, deliverable variant

**Version**:
One numbered revision of a Variant, made by the Creative Team for review.
_Avoid_: Draft, iteration

**Version file**:
The exact exported video or image submitted as a Version: the file Marketing reviews and approves and that Launches use.
_Avoid_: Final file, master, completed file, deliverable

**Working file**:
A file the Creative Team edits from, such as an editing project, linked from a Variant but never reviewed or approved.
_Avoid_: Source file

**Footage**:
A batch of unedited material from one shoot session or one creator's delivery, kept in one Drive folder and reusable across Variants.
_Avoid_: Raw files, rushes, assets, 初稿 (the rough material attached to a Brief)

**Footage Code**:
The permanent, never-reused identifier of Footage, e.g. `RAW-0012`.

**Production Stage**:
Where a Variant's next Version is in production: for video 叙述方式, rough cut (粗剪), fine cut (精剪) and ready; for statics concept, layout, refinement and ready.
_Avoid_: Status (the Variant's overall state is separate)

**Content Section**:
A timed part of one video Version, such as Hook, Body or CTA. Optional detail labels describe its purpose, such as problem, demo, proof or offer. This is separate from the Production Stage of the work.

**Transcript**:
The words spoken in an exact video Version, stored with timestamps and language. On-screen text is recorded separately from speech. Corrections keep their history.

**Live Variant**:
A Variant that has been posted or advertised; its file can no longer change, so any further change is a new Variant.

**Retired Variant**:
A Live Variant with no live Ad and no planned Launch.

**Cancelled Variant**:
A Variant abandoned before it went live.

### Labels

**Product**:
A saved product or service that a Brief promotes.

**Initiative**:
An optional saved business campaign a Brief belongs to, such as an 11.11 or Raya promotion.
_Avoid_: Campaign (a Meta campaign is a different thing)

**Avatar**:
A saved customer persona that a Creative is aimed at.
_Avoid_: Persona, target audience, presenter (people on screen are not Avatars)

**Angle**:
The saved marketing argument a Creative makes to its Avatar.
_Avoid_: Message, concept, theme

**Format**:
Whether a Creative is a video, a static image or a carousel.

**Funnel Stage**:
The saved stage a Creative is made for: awareness, consideration or conversion.

**Narrative Style**:
The saved way a Creative is told (叙述方式), such as testimonial, Q&A, story or demo.
_Avoid_: Format, template, storytelling

**Hook**:
The opening Content Section of a video, chosen by the Creative Team and labelled with a hook type; a different Hook makes a different Variant.

### Review

**Review**:
One pass by the Marketing Team over a single Version, ending in an explicit Approve or Request Changes decision.

**Comment**:
Feedback attached to an exact Version, optionally at a video timestamp, image position or carousel card, marked either blocking or a suggestion (建议).

**Blocking Comment**:
A Comment marked must-fix (必改); while one is unresolved on a Variant, none of its Versions can be approved.

**Approval Withdrawal**:
The Approver's recorded reversal of an approval, with a reason, after which no new Launch may use that Version.

### Launching

**Launch**:
One showing of a Variant to people as a Post or an Ad, normally planned for an approved Variant and live once confirmed; an Ad seen running without a plan is recorded as an unplanned live Launch.

**Post**:
An organic publication of a Live Variant on a page or account.
_Avoid_: Organic ad, publication

**Ad**:
A paid Meta ad running a Live Variant, named with that Variant's code; it may promote an existing Post.
_Avoid_: Boost (a boost is an Ad that promotes a Post)

**Untracked Ad**:
A Meta ad whose name carries no recognisable code, waiting to be linked to a Variant.

**Mismatched Ad**:
An Ad whose name carries a Variant's code but whose Meta video differs from the one recorded for that Variant, waiting for someone to confirm.

**Dynamic Ad**:
An Ad that rotates several Variants inside one ad, so its results count only at ad level.

### Results

**Result**:
The outcome an ad set is optimised for, such as a lead, a message or a purchase, as Meta reports it (成效).
_Avoid_: Conversion, KPI

**Cost per Result**:
Spend divided by Results, and the final judge of a Variant's or Combination's performance (单次成效费用).
_Avoid_: CPA, CPL (each names only one kind of Result)

**Combination**:
The Live Variants running together in one ad set over a period, judged as a whole because Meta decides how spend is split between them.
_Avoid_: Ad set (the Meta container itself), mix

**Breakdown**:
A split of an Ad's numbers by one audience or placement dimension, such as age, gender or placement (细分).

**Controlled Test**:
A comparison run as a Meta A/B test; every other comparison is observational.
_Avoid_: A/B test (for two ads that merely share an ad set)

### Learning

**Creative Retrospective (复盘)**:
The two teams' review of what a Creative tried to achieve, its exact video and sections, what the results show, and what to change next. It is the analysis inside an Optimization Plan, separate from the pre-launch approval Review.

**Finding**:
One observation or hypothesis recorded in a Creative Retrospective, about the whole video, a Content Section or a time range, and marked keep, change or test.
_Avoid_: Note, insight, learning

**Optimization Plan**:
A plan both teams write after reviewing results, made of Evidence, a hypothesis and actions, which later receives a Verdict.
_Avoid_: Learning record, experiment, report, performance review

**Evidence**:
The numbers, exact Version file and annotations an Optimization Plan cites, frozen when the plan is written so later corrections cannot change them.

**Verdict**:
The conclusion recorded on an Optimization Plan at its check date: confirmed, not confirmed, or not enough data.
